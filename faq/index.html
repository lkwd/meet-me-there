Getting started

What is Meet Me There?

Meet Me There is a yacht-life roleplay experience on Roblox with fashion at its heart. Hang out with friends, battle on the dance floor, work a shift at the bar, explore the yacht and the island, and build your style and status as more of the yacht opens up.

How do I start playing?

Meet Me There is played on Roblox, so all you need is a Roblox account. Tap Play on this site, or search for Meet Me There in the Roblox app.

When does it open?

All aboard on 9 November 2026. Join our Discord or Roblox group to hear about playtests, news and events first.

Why isn't everything open yet?

On purpose. The yacht opens up over time, with new areas, experiences and events arriving regularly. Some of them will be shaped by player votes and feedback.

Purchases & Robux

I bought something and didn't receive it. What should I do?

First, rejoin the experience, as purchases sometimes take a moment to appear. If it's still missing, contact us with your Roblox username, the item you bought and roughly when you bought it.

How do refunds and billing work?

Purchases in Meet Me There are made with Robux, and Roblox handles all Robux payments and billing. If you have a question about a charge or want to ask about a refund, contact Roblox Support.

Can I set a limit on how much is spent?

Yes. Roblox's Parental Controls let a parent set a monthly spending limit and choose which purchase notifications they get. See managing spending in the For parents section.

Safety & your account

How do I report another player?

Use Roblox's built-in report tool: open the Roblox menu in the experience, select Report, and choose the player. You can also block players from the same menu.

What are the rules on board?

Everyone in Meet Me There follows the Roblox Community Standards. Be kind, respect other players' stories and outfits, and report anyone who makes the yacht a worse place to be.

I can't get into my account, or I think it's been hacked.

Your account belongs to Roblox, so Roblox Support is the right place to recover it. We can't access or change Roblox accounts. To protect your account, turn on 2-Step Verification in your Roblox security settings and never share your password, even with someone claiming to be staff.

My Roblox account was banned. Can you help?

Account bans and moderation decisions are made by Roblox, so appeals go through Roblox Support.

For parents and carers

A guide for parents

Meet Me There is an experience on Roblox, so your child plays with their Roblox account and Roblox's safety settings apply. You can manage spending, chat and the experiences they can join from Roblox's Parental Controls. Roblox's parental controls guide has step-by-step instructions.

Is Meet Me There suitable for my child?

Every Roblox experience shows a content maturity label on its Roblox page. Check the label on Meet Me There's page, then use Parental Controls to set the maturity level your child can access, or to block specific experiences.

How do I manage what my child spends?

In Roblox, go to Settings, then Parental Controls, and choose your child's account. Under spending restrictions you can set a monthly spending limit and choose which purchase notifications you get.

Can I control who my child chats with?

Yes. In Parental Controls, the communication settings let you turn experience chat and direct chat on or off, and manage chat and voice chat with friends. You can also review your child's connections and block or report users.

How do I set up Parental Controls?

Link a parent account to your child's account: from your child's account, open Parental Controls and choose to add a parent, then follow the steps to verify your age. Once linked, you can manage their settings from your own account.

I have a concern about something in Meet Me There.

Report anything that happens in the experience using Roblox's Report tool, which goes straight to Roblox's moderation team. If your concern is about Meet Me There itself, contact us.

Community & feedback

How can I share feedback or join a playtest?

Join our Discord, where we share playtests, run community votes and collect feedback. You can also contact us at any time.

How do I join the Founding Crew?

Join our Discord or Roblox group, where we'll share how to get involved. Spaces are limited.

How old do I need to be to join the Discord?

Discord requires members to be at least 13, or older in some countries. See Discord's Terms of Service. If you're younger, you can follow news through our Roblox group instead.

Still need help?

Something not working? Get in touch with our support team. Include your Roblox username and a quick description of the issue so we can help.
