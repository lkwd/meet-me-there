Welcome to Meet Me There, a new roleplay experience on Roblox with fashion at its heart.

Dress up, meet your friends, hit the dance floor, or head out on the water. There's a whole yacht to explore, and what happens next is up to you.

Life Aboard

Dance battles? A trip to the island? Just here for the vibes? There's no set way to spend your time on board. Find your thing and make yourself at home.

On board

Think you've got the moves? Take on your friends in a Dance Battle. Hit the steps on cue, build your combos and see who comes out on top.

Coming aboard

Fashion

Got a look worth showing off? Get ready to style, strut and take to the catwalk. Fashion is getting its moment on deck, and you'll want to be there.

On board

The Juice Club

Smoothies, anyone? Get behind the bar, take orders, and earn Coins while you work. Or grab a seat, catch up with friends, and enjoy the view.

On board

Explore

Fancy getting away from the crowd? Hop on the jet tender and head to the island. Follow the stepping stones, explore your surroundings, and collect your daily reward.

Roleplay & Hang Out

Who are you going to be today? Pick a role, put together a look, and make your own story. Or find a spot on deck and meet someone new.

Your voyage. Your day. Your rules.

There's no right way to experience Meet Me There. Here's a taste of what you can get up to.

  1. 01

    Arrive

    First stop, the island. Make your way across the Floating Port and head aboard. Your yacht life starts here.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Take a look around the yacht or hop on the tender and head out to the island. There's plenty to discover.

  3. 03

    Find Your Scene

    Dance floor, bar, open water or roleplay with friends? Whatever you're in the mood for, there's a place for you.

  4. 04

    Get Rewarded

    Earn XP, collect Coins and unlock achievements as you play. There's always something to work towards.
